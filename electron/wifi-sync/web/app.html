Add MiLeo to your Home Screen

    More about Wi-Fi and the certificate

    Stay on the same Wi-Fi as your Desktop and keep MiLeo Photo Library running there. Photos and sync need a live Desktop connection; offline support stores only the app shell, never your photos.

    The Desktop uses a self-signed HTTPS certificate. Only trust it after comparing its fingerprint with the Desktop. Full app mode and offline support need the phone to trust it; the Home Screen shortcut works either way.
